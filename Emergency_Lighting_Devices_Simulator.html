Important: “Full output” in bypass mode applies to a compatible line-voltage control. A 0–10V or digital dimming system may need a separately listed dimming-disconnect/override function. A UL 1008 transfer switch does not automatically override a downstream wall switch. Listing alone does not establish suitability for the selected wiring method, voltage, inverter, or installation.
